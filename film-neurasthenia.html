About the Film

An Iranian in his mid-20s struggles with everyday life. He is stricken with overpowering tiredness, everything is too difficult.

He can't sleep, has neither strength nor courage and spends the night in his car. Life parked in a dead-end. Visually stunning and with an almost documentary feel.
